1. INTRODUCTION

Expense Tracker / Quản Lý Chi Tiêu ("the App") is a personal expense-management application developed by NGUYEN QUOC TUAN ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how that information is used, where it may be processed, and the choices available to you.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. INFORMATION THE APP ACCESSES

Depending on the features you use, the App may access or process:

  • Notification information, if you enable Android Notification Access.
  • Transaction information such as amount, currency, merchant, category, transaction date/time, and transaction description.
  • Information that you manually enter into the App.
  • Exchange-rate information required for currency-conversion features.
  • Technical information necessary for the App to operate and communicate with its services.

3. NOTIFICATION ACCESS

The App can optionally use Android Notification Access to detect financial transactions from notifications displayed by supported banking, payment, card, or financial-service applications.

If you enable Notification Access, the App may process notification information such as:

  • Notification title and text;
  • Source application name or identifier;
  • Notification timestamp;
  • Transaction amount;
  • Currency;
  • Merchant or transaction description;
  • Other transaction-related text contained in the notification.

The purpose is to identify potential income or expense transactions and automatically record them in the App.

Notification Access is not required for the App's basic manual expense-management functionality.

The App does not intentionally use Notification Access to read private communications for advertising, sell notification data, or publicly disclose notification content.

Because notifications can contain sensitive information, you should only enable Notification Access if you understand and accept this processing.

You can disable Notification Access at any time through Android system settings.

4. GOOGLE GEMINI

The App uses Google's Gemini API for AI-assisted transaction analysis and categorization.

When this functionality is used, relevant information may be transmitted to Google's Gemini API. Depending on the notification, this may include relevant notification text, application/source name, merchant information, transaction amount, currency, and other transaction-related information.

The purpose is to determine whether notification content represents a financial transaction and/or assist with transaction categorization.

The App is not intended to send banking passwords, PINs, authentication codes, or other credentials to Gemini. However, if such information is included in notification content that you allow the App to access, it may be present in the content processed by the AI service.

We recommend that you carefully review the notification sources you allow the App to access.

Google may process information submitted to Gemini according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. FINANCIAL AND TRANSACTION INFORMATION

The App may process financial information because its purpose is personal expense management.

This may include:

  • Income and expense amounts;
  • Currency;
  • Merchant names or descriptions;
  • Transaction categories;
  • Transaction dates and times;
  • Notes or descriptions entered by you;
  • Remittance or currency-conversion records.

This information is used to provide transaction recording, categorization, budgeting, statistics, and related features.

The App is not a bank and does not provide banking or payment-processing services.

6. LOCAL STORAGE

The App stores transaction and application data locally on your device as necessary for its functionality.

Locally stored information may include transaction records, categories, category rules, currency information, budget information, and application preferences.

The App does not require an account for its core expense-management functionality.

If you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App, locally stored data may be deleted. You are responsible for maintaining any backup you need.

7. EXCHANGE-RATE SERVICES

The App may communicate with third-party exchange-rate services to obtain currency exchange information.

Exchange-rate requests are used to provide currency conversion and related functionality.

8. DATA SHARING

We do not sell your personal or financial information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide features you use, including:

  • Google Gemini, for AI-assisted transaction analysis and categorization;
  • Exchange-rate service providers, for obtaining exchange-rate information.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, security, or prevent fraud or abuse.

9. DATA RETENTION

Transaction information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or the operating system removes it.

We do not intentionally maintain notification content on our own servers as part of the App's normal local transaction-management functionality.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the applicable third party's retention policies.

PROMINENT DISCLOSURE – NOTIFICATION ACCESS

Why does Expense Tracker need access to your notifications?

Expense Tracker can read notifications from apps on your device to automatically detect financial transactions such as money received or money spent.

When Notification Access is enabled, the App may process notification information such as notification text, application/source name, transaction amount, currency, merchant name, timestamp, and other transaction-related information.

Some relevant notification information may be sent to Google Gemini to help determine whether a notification represents a financial transaction and/or assist with transaction categorization.

This information is used to provide the automatic transaction-recording and categorization features.

Notification Access is optional. You can continue using the App's basic manual expense-management features without enabling this permission.

By selecting "Allow", you confirm that you understand and agree to the use of notification information as described above.
