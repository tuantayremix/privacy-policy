🐷 Privacy Policy

App: Quản Lý Chi Tiêu (Expense Tracker)  |  Package name: com.expensetracker.app  |  Last updated: October 2026

"Quản Lý Chi Tiêu" ("the App", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects information when you use it on your Android device.

🔔 PROMINENT DISCLOSURE – NOTIFICATION ACCESS

Why does Expense Tracker need access to your notifications?

Expense Tracker can read notifications from apps on your device to automatically detect financial transactions such as money received or money spent.

When Notification Access is enabled, the App may process notification information such as notification text, application/source name, transaction amount, currency, merchant name, timestamp, and other transaction-related information.

Some relevant notification information may be sent to Google Gemini (and, as a fallback, OpenAI) to help determine whether a notification represents a financial transaction and/or assist with transaction categorization.

This information is used to provide the automatic transaction-recording and categorization features. It is not used for advertising purposes and is not sold to third parties.

Notification Access is optional. You can continue using the App's basic manual expense-management features without granting this permission. If you do not grant it, you will need to enter transactions manually.

1. Data We Process

Personal financial data (balance, transactions, savings goals, budgets)

All of this data is stored locally on your device (an internal SQLite database). We do not upload this data to any server or cloud service operated by us. If you uninstall the App, all this data is permanently deleted from your device.

Bank notification content (Notification Access)

The App uses the Notification Listener permission solely to read the content of transaction notifications from banking/e-wallet apps installed on your device, in order to automatically record transactions (amount, description, remaining balance) into your expense log — so you don't have to enter data manually.

The App does not read, store, or share notifications unrelated to financial transactions (social media messages, personal emails, OTP codes, etc.). Notification content is processed on-device and/or sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to a third-party AI service (see Section 2) solely to categorize the transaction; the result is then stored locally on your device only.

2. Third-Party Data Sharing

To automatically categorize transactions (e.g. identifying an expense as food, shopping, or savings), the App sends the text of the transaction notification (without your name, phone number, or other personal identifiers) to the following AI services:

We do not sell user data to third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier and certain device data to serve relevant ads. You can manage ad personalization preferences in your device's Google Settings.

5. Data Security

Your financial data is stored locally, protected by Android's per-app sandboxing. All data sent externally (to the categorization API) is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Since your data is stored only on your device, you have full control: delete individual transactions within the App, or uninstall the App to immediately erase all data from your device.
